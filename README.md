ExpenseFlowA mobile-first expense reporting and approval workflow for teams that need clearer policy control, faster decisions, and finance-ready exports.ExpenseFlow is an Expo and React Native application that lets employees capture receipts, prepare expense reports, receive policy feedback before submission, and follow a transparent multi-level approval route. Managers and finance teams can review pending reports, monitor category budgets, inspect operational metrics, and prepare approved expenses for export to accounting systems.HighlightsCapability What it does
Receipt capture and import Captures a receipt with the device camera or imports an existing receipt image, then presents editable report details.
Policy compliance Checks for receipt thresholds, meal caps, high-value travel, missing business purpose, and category budget overages.
Expense workflow Supports drafts, submission, expense detail review, policy findings, and clear report-status visibility.
Multi-level approvals Routes reports through manager and finance approval stages, with approve, return, reject, and decision-note actions.
Budget tracking Shows category allocations, actual spend, forecast variance, remaining budget, and categories at risk.
Finance insights Summarizes spend by category, reporting health, policy exceptions, accounting-export status, and approval speed.
Accounting readiness Models QuickBooks, Xero, and NetSuite mappings, export readiness, and post-approval export status.
Local-first data Persists workflow changes securely on the device through AsyncStorage, allowing the experience to run without a backend.Experience FlowCopyCapture or import receipt
